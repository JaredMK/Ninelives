How to read the power column

  • Bonus Coins — expected coins per firing/deal: value × chance, or a per-unit rate with the unit named (typical units: pile size 5, 6 landings per column per deal, 72-card deck, streak 3).
  • Coin Drains — coins lost per firing.
  • Peeks — cards peeked per firing/use.
  • Tells — higher/lower/same hints armed per firing/use.
  • Shuffles / Buries / Donates — piles or cards affected per firing/use.
  • Guards / Saves / Revives — effective save strength: chance × what is saved (share of draws made safe, or save chance on death).
  • Deck Edits — scope and control: rank steps / suits matched, 1 card vs a column; controlled before random, guaranteed before chance-based.
  • Score Shaping — pile-size points added (or variable product effects, which sort last).
  • Item & Store Meta — activations granted or store value per use, units named per row.
  • Packs — revealed × kept.
