SHOULDA SAID SAME — FIRST-RUN TUTORIAL TEXT (TUT2, guided + gated)
===================================================================
Edit freely and hand back — I'll apply it verbatim.
- *asterisks* mark the pink BOLD words in a bubble.
- Line breaks inside a TEXT block are real line breaks in the bubble.
- BUTTON is the label on the advance button ("Next" if unchanged).
- Every bubble also carries the small "Skip tips" link (label below).
  Skip ends the WHOLE tour instantly: all gating removed, marked seen.
- GATE lines describe the hard input gate active around a step: while a
  gate holds, ONLY the named control accepts input.
- The tour is marked seen (ninelives.pref.tutorial2) only when it
  COMPLETES (the final battle deal starts) or on Skip — losing the run
  mid-tour restarts it on the next fresh campaign.

GLOBAL
  SKIP LINK LABEL: Skip tips
  DEFAULT BUTTON:  Next


STEP 1 — pinky
  SCREEN:    Deck select (the first Play)
  POINTS AT: The big Pinky character
  BUTTON:    Let's go
  TEXT:
  This is *Pinky* — he's lost, a long way from mama.
  Help him climb home.


STEP 2 — map goal
  SCREEN:    Map (first visit)
  POINTS AT: Pinky's avatar at the bottom of the trail
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Pinky starts down here and must climb up.


STEP 3 — the loop (deck growth)
  SCREEN:    Map (immediately after step 2)
  POINTS AT: A card/pack node (or Pinky if none is visible)
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  *Card nodes* grow Pinky's deck as he climbs — and a bigger deck means *longer, harder deals*.
  Drafting well and shopping for upgrades is how you keep up.


STEP 4 — first node
  SCREEN:    Map (immediately after step 3)
  POINTS AT: The one glowing (guided) first deal node
  GATE:      Only this node is tappable; every other legal node is dimmed and inert
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  Tap the *glowing node* to set off to your first deal.


STEP 5 — survive
  SCREEN:    First deal (as the board appears)
  POINTS AT: The board of piles
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  *Goal: survive.* Every pile is a life.


STEP 6 — how to guess
  SCREEN:    First deal
  POINTS AT: A pile
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Pick a pile, then *swipe*:
  ↑ higher
  ↓ lower
  sideways for same.
  Or tap the pile and use the buttons on the left


STEP 7 — the deck
  SCREEN:    First deal
  POINTS AT: The deck character (Pinky in the corner)
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Every guess draws from *Pinky's deck*.
  Correct guess → the pile grows
  Wrong guess → the pile dies
  Beat the *whole deck* before every pile is gone.


STEP 8 — same shield
  SCREEN:    First deal
  POINTS AT: The Same-shield chip in the HUD
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  *Same* guesses are hard. A correct Same call charges this *shield*, which saves your next miss.


STEP 9 — your turn
  SCREEN:    First deal (hands control to the player; guessing is never gated)
  POINTS AT: (nothing — centered)
  BUTTON:    Go
  TEXT:
  Your turn — make a guess!


STEP 10 — coins
  SCREEN:    First deal summary (Deal cleared)
  POINTS AT: The deal reward line, surviving piles line, and smallest pile line of the coin breakdown
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Coins = *surviving piles × smallest pile*
  Spend coins on upgrades.


STEP 11 — where coins live
  SCREEN:    First deal summary (immediately after step 10)
  POINTS AT: The summary's own "Coins held" running-balance row (the summary
             overlay covers the HUD bar, so the ring lands on a balance the
             player can actually see)
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  Your *coins* bank into this balance — the same counter rides the top bar on every screen.


STEP 12 — to the shop
  SCREEN:    Map (after the first payout)
  POINTS AT: The one glowing node on the guided path to the shop
  GATE:      Only that node is tappable
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  Shops along the trail sell *upgrades* — let's visit one.
  Follow the *glowing node*.

  (FALLBACK-PATH INTERMEDIATES — when the guided path has stops before
   the shop/draft, each gated stop gets ONE short bubble by node type:)
    deal:   A *deal* blocks the path — survive it and press on.
    store:  Another *shop* on the way — pass through.
    pack:   A *card pack* on the way — take it.
    pickup: A free *card* on the way — take it.


  (GRANT CLAUSE — whenever a forced step tops the balance up, its bubble
   gains this line, paired with a coin tick + a pulse on both counters:)
    A few coins *on the house* to cover it.


STEP 13 — the shop: your balance
  SCREEN:    Guided shop visit
  POINTS AT: The shop coin balance (#storeCoins)
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Welcome to the shop. Your *coins* to spend sit here — the same balance as the top bar.
  (+ the grant clause when the sticker step topped the balance up)


STEP 14 — stickers + the forced Bonus Coin buy
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (immediately after step 13)
  POINTS AT: The Bonus Coin sticker slot (always present in this first shelf)
  GATE:      Only that slot is tappable; if coins fall short of its live
             items.js price, the exact shortfall is granted first
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  *Stickers* attach to *one card* and ride it for the whole run.
  This *Bonus Coin* pays *+1 coin* — tap it to buy.
  (the item name and its payout read LIVE from items.js — gainCoin's
   label and description — so a data edit never leaves this bubble stale)


STEP 15 — stick it on a heart
  SCREEN:    The buy-time card picker (#stickerApplyModal)
  POINTS AT: The sticker banner at the top of the picker
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  This sticker is *♥ only* — other suits grey out.
  Tap a *heart*, then confirm.


STEP 16 — refresh
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (after the sticker lands)
  POINTS AT: The Refresh button (#rerollBtn)
  GATE:      Only Refresh is tappable; a shortfall against the live reroll
             cost is granted first
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  *Refresh* re-rolls the whole shelf for coins — the price climbs each use within a visit.
  Tap *Refresh*.
  (+ the grant clause when this step topped the balance up)


STEP 17 — pillars + the forced Guardian buy
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (the post-refresh shelf always carries the Guardian)
  POINTS AT: The Guardian pillar slot
  GATE:      Only that slot is tappable; shortfall granted as above
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  *Pillars* bind to the top of a column and work passively every deal.
  The *Guardian* pays out when every pile in its column survives — tap it and pick a column.
  (+ the grant clause when this step topped the balance up)


STEP 18 — bases (explain only)
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (after the Guardian is placed)
  POINTS AT: The shop shelf
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  Two more kinds live on this shelf.
  *Bases* sit at a column's foot — a once-per-deal power you tap during play.


STEP 19 — same-powers (explain only)
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (immediately after step 18)
  POINTS AT: The shop shelf
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  *Same-Powers* equip to the Same button — exactly one at a time, firing on every correct *Same* call.


STEP 20 — leave the shop
  SCREEN:    Guided shop (immediately after step 19)
  POINTS AT: The GO TO MAP button (#storeStartBtn)
  GATE:      Only GO TO MAP is tappable
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  All stocked up — tap *GO TO MAP* to head back out.


STEP 21 — draft a card
  SCREEN:    Map (back from the shop)
  POINTS AT: The one glowing node on the guided path to the +1 card
  GATE:      Only that node is tappable
  BUTTON:    Got it
  TEXT:
  Travel on to *draft new cards* — every card node grows Pinky's deck, and a bigger deck means harder deals… but more power to win with.
  Grab the *+1 card* ahead.


STEP 22 — the deck grew
  SCREEN:    Map (the drafted card just flew into the deck)
  POINTS AT: The deck count in the map header (#deckCount)
  BUTTON:    Next
  TEXT:
  The deck just *grew* — Pinky now carries *N cards*.   (N = live deck size)
  More cards = longer deals, but more to win with.


STEP 23 — battle (ends the tour)
  SCREEN:    Map (immediately after step 22)
  POINTS AT: The one glowing deal node
  GATE:      Only that deal is tappable; the tour COMPLETES (marked seen,
             all gating removed) the moment the deal starts
  BUTTON:    Go
  TEXT:
  Time to *battle* — tap the deal. Good luck!
