SHOULDA SAID SAME — HOW TO PLAY
===============================

This file mirrors the in-game "How to Play" walkthrough (the slides shown
from the main menu / in-game menu). It is a guided, slide-by-slide
walkthrough — not a wall of text. Each slide has a TITLE, a short BODY, and
a small VISUAL (described here in brackets for context).

Source in code: showManual() in index.html (the `slides` array).
Emphasis below uses *asterisks* where the game bolds a word.


SLIDE 1 — This is your board
----------------------------
[Visual: a grid of 6 face-up cards (piles), e.g. 7♠ K♥ 4♣ / A♦ 9♠ Q♥]

A grid of *piles*. Each pile shows one *card* on top. These are what you
keep alive.


SLIDE 2 — Work through the deck
-------------------------------
[Visual: a 52-card deck → arrow → a small board of 4 cards]

You have a *deck* of cards. Each turn you draw the next one and *place it*
on a pile by guessing. Your goal is to get through the *whole deck*.


SLIDE 3 — Make a guess
----------------------
[Visual: a 7♥ above three buttons: HIGHER / SAME / LOWER]

Tap a pile, then call the next card: will it be *Higher*, *Same*, or
*Lower* than the top card? (Aces are high; suits don't matter.)


SLIDE 4 — Grow it or lose it
----------------------------
[Visual: left "Correct" — a growing stack (+1); right "Wrong" — a dead pile (✕)]

Guess *right* and the card joins the pile, so it grows taller. Guess
*wrong* and the whole pile *dies*.


SLIDE 5 — Clear the deck, earn coins
------------------------------------
[Visual: 4 (piles alive) × 3 (smallest pile) = 12 coins]

Survive the whole *deck* to win the run. You earn *surviving piles × your
smallest pile* in coins, so keep every pile alive and evenly sized, not
over-stacked.


SLIDE 6 — Spend between runs
----------------------------
[Visual: Sticker (3 coins) → card with a badge; Pillar (6 coins) → a column]

In the *store*, buy *stickers*, *pillars* and packs with coins, then place
them: a sticker goes on a *card*, a pillar locks onto a whole *column*.
Then start your run.


NOTES FOR EDITING
-----------------
- To change wording, edit the `cap` (caption) of each slide in showManual().
  The caption format is: '<span class="lead">TITLE</span>' + "BODY (HTML, may
  use <b>...</b> for emphasis)".
- To add/remove a slide, add/remove an entry in the `slides` array (each entry
  is { art: "<...visual HTML...>", cap: "<...caption HTML...>" }); the dot
  navigation and Back/Next/Skip/Done buttons adapt automatically to the count.
- The visuals (`art`) are small inline HTML mockups (mini-cards, arrows, the
  HIGHER/SAME/LOWER buttons, the store items, the score expression).
